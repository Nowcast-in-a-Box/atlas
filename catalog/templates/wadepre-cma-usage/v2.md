# WADEPre · CMA demo

Cross-domain demo: SEVIR pretrained WADEPre weights on CMA CREF. Input dBZ is clipped to 0..75 and normalized to 0..1; the 20 five-minute outputs are resampled to 20 six-minute leads. Forecast skill is unvalidated.

Use July 2026 continuous CMA samples. Target grid: 128x128 at 3000 m in a local LAEA projection. Outputs remain under runs/<id>/.
